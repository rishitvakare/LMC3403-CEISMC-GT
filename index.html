01 / The opportunity gap

Access to school
is only the
beginning.

Attending school does not guarantee equal opportunities to explore STEM. Students have different levels of access to learning materials, hands-on activities, academic support, and enrichment programs.

These differences can shape their confidence, interests, and future pathways. Connecting schools with additional resources helps educators expand meaningful learning opportunities for their students.

HIGH SCHOOLS OFFERING CALCULUS · 2020–2021

High Black & Latino enrollment35%
Low Black & Latino enrollment54%
19 percentage points separate access to the same subject.

The national picture

Unequal access. Unequal starting points.

In 2020–2021, 35% of high schools with predominantly Black and Latino students offered calculus, compared with 54% of schools with low Black and Latino enrollment. Where students attend school can shape their STEM opportunities.

Source: U.S. Department of Education · See references ↗

The Georgia connection

University expertise, closer to home.

CEISMC and STEM@GTRI’s Rural Computer Science Initiative has reached more than 14,000 students across 60 Georgia school districts, connecting rural schools with computer science learning and university expertise.

The long-term possibility

Early experiences can shape future choices.

A Maryland study estimated that taking a high school computer science course increased the likelihood of earning a CS bachelor’s degree by approximately 5 percentage points. Meaningful opportunities today can open educational pathways tomorrow.

03 / Your classroom, connected

A bridge between
Georgia Tech
and your school.

The Center for Education Integrating Science, Mathematics, and Computing (CEISMC), part of Georgia Tech’s College of Lifetime Learning, connects Georgia Tech with K–12 communities across Georgia and beyond.

Through hands-on learning, teacher development, strategic partnerships, and research and evaluation, CEISMC expands access to STEM/STEAM education, supports student success, and strengthens the future workforce.
